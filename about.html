About Idorenyin Bassey Inyang

Entrepreneur, real estate developer, and cybersecurity practitioner committed to building physical communities and securing digital systems.

Professional Profile & Aspirations

As the Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer of Digiforte Prime Properties Ltd, I oversee residential layout developments, infrastructure delivery, and property transactions across Abuja, Nigeria. Beyond real estate, I am currently expanding my technical mastery through the undergraduate Cybersecurity program at Miva Open University.

My passion for cybersecurity stems from a core belief: in an increasingly connected world, protecting physical assets without securing digital data creates systemic vulnerability. Cybersecurity offers an avenue to safeguard resources, enforce access control, and preserve digital trust across both corporate enterprise and PropTech ecosystems.
